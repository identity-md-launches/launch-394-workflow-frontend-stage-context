  - region "How the vault behaves":
    - heading "How the vault behaves" [level=2]
    - list:
      - listitem: Donations vest linearly over seven days. A new donation merges the unvested remainder and restarts the seven-day clock.
      - listitem: PIGY sent to the vault by a plain transfer counts immediately and is not streamed. Use Donate for a streamed gift.
      - listitem: If every share is redeemed while donations are still vesting, the remainder accrues to the next depositors.
      - listitem: Rounding follows ERC-4626 and always favours the vault. A deposit too small to mint a share reverts.
      - listitem: Withdrawals pay the connected wallet only. There is no owner, fee, pause or upgrade path.
